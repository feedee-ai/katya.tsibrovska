OWN-WORLD: Slate black ground (warm #15110f), chalk cream type, the slate's colour-bar strip as the palette: copper of her hair (#c4511f), cherry, butter, teal-grey. Diagonal clapper stripes as the only ornament. Unbounded wide caps for headlines, Golos Text for reading, Marck Script as the marker filling slate fields. Call-sheet paper (cream) for the service rows.

Then: TikTok is a system → three doors (business / learning / shoots) on a call sheet → generator proves the method on their niche → cases → takes (reels) → about → process → quotes → FAQ → form.
